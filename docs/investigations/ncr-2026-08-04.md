# Missing NCR report, 4–10 Aug 2026

**Verdict: the DOE has not published it. Discovery did not miss it and the
import did not reject it — as of 7 Aug 2026 the document does not exist.**

## How this was established

The CMS was queried directly, not inferred from our own logs.

```graphql
documents(siteKey: "guest", flatten: true, pageSize: 100, page: N, sort: "dateModified:desc")
```

1. The 1,500 newest documents in the library were scanned. **Zero** carry both
   `NCR` and any spelling of the 4 Aug week (`0804`, `08042026`, `August 4`,
   `Aug 4`) in the title.
2. The newest NCR liquid-fuels document in the entire library is
   `NCR Price Monitoring 07282026.pdf`, `dateModified 2026-08-02T11:47:06Z` —
   the 28 Jul week, which we hold.
3. The 4 Aug week *is* present for the other region:
   `VFO PRICE MONITORING 080426_with LGU and Field.pdf`,
   `dateModified 2026-08-07T13:19:09Z` — which we also hold.

The three candidate explanations resolve as:

| GraphQL discovery failed | Ruled out — the document is absent from the source, and the run discovered 9 documents including the same week's Regions 6-8 file |

## The cadence, which is the actual finding

Each series publishes a fixed number of days after its week begins:

| Week begins | NCR published | Lag | Regions 6-8 published | Lag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 Jul | 12 Jul | 5 days | 10 Jul | 3 days |
| 14 Jul | 19 Jul | 5 days | 17 Jul | 3 days |
| 21 Jul | 26 Jul | 5 days | 24 Jul | 3 days |
| 28 Jul | 2 Aug | 5 days | 31 Jul | 3 days |
| 4 Aug | *not yet* | — | 7 Aug | 3 days |

NCR runs a consistent **five-day** lag and Regions 6-8 a consistent
**three-day** one. The NCR report for the 4 Aug week is therefore due around
**9 Aug 2026** and is not overdue today.

**Operational consequence:** for roughly two days of every week — from the
Regions 6-8 publication until the NCR one — NCR will legitimately trail by one
publication, and the freshness panel will say so. That is the system working,
not a fault. It becomes a real fault if NCR exceeds the five-day lag by a wide
margin; the runbook uses **nine days since the covered week began** as the
point at which to investigate.

## Reproducing this check

`scripts/probe_doe_library.py` runs the same query. It is read-only and needs
no credentials.

```bash
python3 scripts/probe_doe_library.py --contains NCR --pages 15
```
